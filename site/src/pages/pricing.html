Billing FAQ

Questions about plans and billing

What is the closed-loop MCP?

A Model Context Protocol server that gives your coding agent what GenClass found on your deployed app: each finding with its diagnosis, confidence, the pages affected and when it was first and last seen, never page text; a check whether a problem came back since a fix; and one prompt that asks the agent to fix the open findings. It reads only what the genclass.dev collector already keeps for your apps, and nothing new is collected for it. It is coming next and is not in the runtime yet. You will run it on your own machine, free, in every plan.

What does hosting it add?

Nothing to run and nothing to keep up to date. Individual gives you one URL on genclass.dev to add to Claude Code, Cursor, Codex or any MCP client, and your agent is prompted when GenClass finds something new on your deployed app, as it is found, with the evidence and the pages affected. After a fix ships, GenClass tells your agent whether the problem came back. Run on your own machine, the same server reads the same findings whenever you start it.

What does Enterprise mean?

The closed-loop MCP deployed inside your own cloud or network, with the collector and the dashboard beside it, so your findings and your code never leave it. It comes with SAML or OIDC sign-in, invoices or purchase orders, your security questionnaire answered and, by arrangement, a GenClass model trained on your own app. There is no card price: email {{plans.contact}} and a person answers within 1 business day.

Is there a free trial?

Yes, of Individual: {{plans.trial_days}} days, no card, once per account. It opens when the hosted MCP ships, since that is what Individual sells. When it ends, the hosted MCP stops and nothing else changes: the dashboard, export and forwarding are free in every plan.

What counts as an app?

One app token. A token groups the diagnostics of one web app on every hostname and route it runs on, so production and staging can share a token or have one each. No plan sets an app limit; to stop abuse an account holds at most {{plans.apps_cap}}, and we raise that if you write to us.

What does the data export include?

Every event GenClass received for your apps, as it arrived: page sessions with versions, mode, device class, hostname and route (ids replaced), model loads, each decision with its diagnosis, confidence and action, detections, actions, undos and the periodic counts, plus when each batch arrived and a two-letter country. It covers events that arrived while the app was in your genclass.dev account and reaches back {{plan.free.history}}. Download it from an app's dashboard as JSONL or CSV, or fetch it with GET /api/account/projects/<id>/events?from=&to= and a bearer token. It holds no page text: GenClass does not collect page text unless your app opts in to situation text, and then only the redacted text the model read. Typed passwords and payment fields, cookies, headers, request and response bodies, IP addresses and user agents are never collected, so they are never in an export.

How does forwarding work?

It is free, and off until you turn it on for an app on its dashboard. Then the genclass.dev collector sends that app's likely detections (not the ones marked possible) to the destinations you connect: Sentry, PostHog, Datadog, Slack, or a webhook whose requests are signed so you can check they came from GenClass. Each record has the diagnosis, confidence, page route, versions and a link to the dashboard, never page text. The same problem on the same page is sent at most once an hour, and an app sends at most 60 records an hour. Nothing is added to your app's code or bundle, data goes only to the tools you connect, and turning forwarding off stops it.

What is interaction analytics?

Reports on how people use your app's pages: clicks, dead clicks and rage clicks, how far they scroll, and the paths they take from page to page. It is being built now and is not in the runtime yet. It will be free when it ships.

Enterprise is invoiced: for an invoice with your company's details, or a purchase order, email {{plans.contact}}.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are by request: email {{plans.contact}} from the address on your billing account. Cancelling stops the next charge, and the plan stays until the end of the month you paid for.

Is there a yearly price?

Not for Individual yet; it is monthly. Enterprise can be paid for a year at once, by invoice. Email {{plans.contact}}.
