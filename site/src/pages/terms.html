Terms

The runtime is Apache-2.0 open source. These terms cover the small services we run around it on genclass.dev.

What these terms cover

The diagnostics collector at genclass.dev/api/v1/events (and its older address on workers.dev), the model files served from genclass.dev/model/, the per-app dashboards at genclass.dev/dashboard/, the optional accounts at genclass.dev/account (with sign-in at genclass.dev/login and the command line sign-in at genclass.dev/login/device), the Individual plan bought at genclass.dev/account/billing, the Enterprise plan arranged by email, and the early-access form. The @genclass/runtime package itself is licensed under Apache-2.0 and these terms do not change that license.

The diagnostics collector

The runtime sends anonymous diagnostics by default from public hosts, as described on the privacy page and in TELEMETRY.md. If you ship GenClass in your app, you decide whether to keep diagnostics on, and you are responsible for telling your own users if your privacy policy or local law requires it. Turning them off takes one option: telemetry: false.

Send only what the runtime sends. Do not use the collector to store anything else, do not send personal data on purpose (for example by opting in to situation text on pages that show names, emails or payment details), and do not send traffic designed to overload it. The collector may rate-limit traffic, and it drops fields it does not know.

Model files and dashboards

The model files are the published @genclass/runtime-model package, mirrored for faster and more reliable loading; you may also self-host them. The per-app dashboards are free and need no plan: the private link shows an app's stats, and an optional account keeps your apps in one place. A dashboard link is private to whoever holds it: anyone with the link can read that app's aggregate counts. Keep it like a password, and ask us to delete a project if a link leaks.

Accounts

An account is optional and free; you sign in with Google, an email and a password, or GitHub, and can add a passkey for extra security. Keep your sign-in methods to yourself: anyone who signs in to your account can see your apps' dashboards and create apps in it, and a command line session you approved can list and create apps in it and read a short summary of each app's diagnostics, but not its dashboard. Approve a code at genclass.dev/login/device only when you ran npx @genclass/runtime login yourself and your terminal shows that code, and revoke sessions you do not recognise on your account page. Accounts are for developers using GenClass; we may suspend one that abuses these services. You can delete yours at any time on your account page. What an account stores is on the privacy page.

Paid plans

Everything GenClass makes is free: the runtime, its on-device model, the dashboard for every app with a year of history, export, forwarding, the page check, and the closed-loop MCP server, which you run on your own machine. What costs money is running the closed-loop MCP for you. Individual is paid monthly, in advance, in US dollars, through Stripe, and goes on sale when the hosted MCP ships; the price is on the pricing page and on your billing page, and the plan renews automatically each month on the date you bought it, until you cancel. Enterprise, the closed-loop MCP inside your own network, is sold by contract: no card, and the price, the invoicing and the term are what the contract says.

Cancel Individual at any time with Manage billing on your billing page. The plan then stays until the end of the month you paid for and does not renew; after that the account is on Free, and the hosted MCP stops. Nothing else changes: the dashboard, export, forwarding, your apps and their data were never part of what you paid for. Refunds are by request: email mehar@daybot.ca from the address on your billing account.

If we change a price, we email the address on your billing account at least 30 days before the new price applies to your next renewal, so you can cancel first. A change to a plan's limits never deletes apps or data you already have.

A trial of Individual lasts {{plans.trial_days}} days, is free, needs no card, and is one per account. It can start once Individual is on sale; it ends on its own and nothing is charged, and when it ends the hosted MCP stops and nothing else changes.

A model trained on your app is part of Enterprise, by arrangement: a person from GenClass agrees with you on what it is trained on and when it ships, and only you receive it. For the closed-loop MCP inside your own network, SSO or invoices, email mehar@daybot.ca.

Retention and deletion

GenClass keeps an app's data, the stored diagnostics and the dashboard aggregates, for 1 year while the app is in a genclass.dev account, and for 90 days for an app known only by its private link. To delete the data for a hostname or a dashboard project sooner, email mehar@daybot.ca.

No warranty

These services, free or paid, are provided as they are, without any warranty, and may change, slow down or stop. GenClass is built so that a missing model, a failed download or an unreachable collector never breaks the app it runs in: the runtime then observes without deciding, or stops sending.
