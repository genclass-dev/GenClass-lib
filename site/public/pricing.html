Billing FAQ

Questions about plans and billing

What counts as an app?

One app token. A token groups the diagnostics of one web app on every hostname and route it runs on, so production and staging can share a token or have one each. Free covers 3 apps. Pro and Custom set no app limit; to stop abuse an account holds at most 200, and we raise that if you write to us.

What does the data export include?

Every event GenClass received from your apps' pages within your plan's history (for the time before you upgraded, the last 90 days), as it arrived: page sessions with versions, mode, device class, hostname and route (ids replaced), model loads, each decision with its diagnosis, confidence and action, detections, actions, undos and the periodic counts, plus when each batch arrived and a two-letter country. Download it from an app's dashboard as JSONL or CSV, or fetch it with GET /api/account/projects/<id>/events?from=&to= and a bearer token. It holds no page text: GenClass does not collect page text unless your app opts in to situation text, and then only the redacted text the model read. Typed passwords and payment fields, cookies, headers, request and response bodies, IP addresses and user agents are never collected, so they are never in an export.

What happens to my history if I downgrade?

When a paid plan ends, the account moves to Free and its dashboards show the last 7 days. GenClass keeps every app's data for at least 90 days on any plan, so upgrading again brings those days back into view. Data older than 90 days is kept for 30 days after the paid plan ends and then deleted, so export what you want to keep within that month. Your apps, tokens and dashboard links keep working.

How does forwarding work?

It is off until you turn it on for an app on its dashboard, on Pro or Custom. Then the genclass.dev collector sends each new detection for that app to the destinations you connect: Sentry, PostHog, Datadog, Slack, or a webhook whose requests are signed so you can check they came from GenClass. Each record has the diagnosis, confidence, page route, versions and a link to the dashboard, never page text. The same problem on the same page is sent at most once an hour. Nothing is added to your app's code or bundle, data goes only to the tools you connect, and turning forwarding off, or the plan ending, stops it.

What does the custom classifier involve, and how long does it take?

After you start Custom, a person from GenClass emails you within 1 business day. Together we pick the bugs that matter in your app and the data to train on: your app's events in GenClass and the bug reports you share with us. We train a GenClass model on them, measure its false reports on your app's normal traffic, and ship it as private model files your runtime loads with model.baseUrl. We aim to ship the first model within 4 weeks of that email; most of that time goes to collecting enough of your app's traffic.

How do taxes and receipts work?

Payments go through Stripe. Checkout adds sales tax or VAT where it applies, and Stripe emails a receipt for each payment. Card details go to Stripe and never reach GenClass. For an invoice with your company's details, or a purchase order, email mehar@daybot.ca.

Can I get a refund?

Yes: email mehar@daybot.ca within 14 days of a charge and we refund it. Custom's first month pays for training work, so once training has started that month is not refunded. Cancelling stops the next charge; the current month is not prorated.

Is there a yearly price?

Not yet; both paid plans are monthly. If your company needs to pay for a year at once, email mehar@daybot.ca.
