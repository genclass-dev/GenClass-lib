Pricing FAQ

Questions about plans

Is the runtime really free for commercial use?

Yes. It is Apache-2.0 open source. Use it in commercial apps with unlimited traffic and no account.

Do you charge per inference or per bug fixed?

No. Inference runs on your users' devices, so it costs nothing to run. Paid plans pay for the team control plane, not for protection.

What happens if a Cloud subscription ends?

Your apps stay protected. The runtime keeps running locally; only the dashboard, alerts and shared controls stop.

What data will Cloud receive?

Only aggregate metrics from apps you connect with a publishable key: counts, categories, timings. No source code, no request bodies, no user state. Cloud is not available yet.
