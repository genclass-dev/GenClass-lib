Self-hosting

TodayThe runtime and its model run in your users' browsers, and you can serve the model from your own origin. The diagnostics collector is open source (telemetry-worker/ in the repository): deploy it to your own Cloudflare account and point your pages at it with telemetry: { endpoint }.

Ask us aboutRunning the dashboard and the paid features in your own cloud.

SSO

TodayAccounts sign in with Google, an email and a password, or GitHub, with passkeys as extra security.

Ask us aboutSAML or OIDC sign-in with your identity provider. It is not built yet; tell us which provider you use.

Invoices

TodayPro ($99 a month) and Custom ($599 a month) are charged monthly to a card through Stripe, with a receipt for each payment.

Ask us aboutPaying by invoice or purchase order, or for a year at once.

Security review

TodayThe runtime is Apache-2.0. Every field it sends is listed under telemetry, the threat model is public, and telemetry: false turns sending off.

Ask us aboutAnswers to your security questionnaire.
