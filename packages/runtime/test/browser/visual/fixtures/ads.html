Daily Example

Council approves the new library budget

The council voted on Tuesday to fund the library's renovation, which will add a reading room and longer opening hours on weekends.

Work starts in the spring. The library stays open during the first phase, with the children's section moving to the community hall.

Residents can comment on the plans until the end of the month at the town hall or by post.

The next council meeting is on the first Tuesday of next month.
