Privacy Policy for Snow Day Predictor

Last updated: February 15, 2026

Snow Day Predictor is available at https://snowday-predictor.ca. This policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and your choices.

Information We Process

When you use the site, your browser may send technical data such as IP address, browser type, referring page, and timestamp through normal web server logs. We use this information for security, reliability, and aggregate usage monitoring.

Input Data

Postal codes and ZIP codes entered into the calculator are used to generate predictions in real time. We do not require account sign-in to use the calculator.

Cookies and Advertising

We may use third-party advertising services, including Google AdSense, which may use cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure ads. You can learn more about Google's advertising technologies here: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Third-Party Services

The site relies on third-party services for weather and geocoding data. Requests to those services are governed by each provider's privacy policy and terms.

Your Choices

You can control cookies through your browser settings. You can also choose not to use the calculator if you do not want to send postal code or ZIP code inputs.

Contact

Consent
