Official Sources for Final Closure Status

📅 February 15, 2026 ✍️ Snow Day Predictor Team ⏱️ 5 min read

Use this page to verify final closure decisions. The calculator gives an estimate, but your official district and transportation channels are the source of truth.

Weather Data

  • Open-Meteo (forecast data used in this tool)
  • Environment and Climate Change Canada
  • U.S. National Weather Service

Ontario Examples

  • Halton District School Board (HDSB)
  • Toronto District School Board (TDSB)
  • Peel District School Board (PDSB)
  • York Region District School Board (YRDSB)
  • Halton Student Transportation Services (HSTS)

United States Guidance

For U.S. districts, verify with:

  • Your district website and district alert system
  • District transportation pages
  • Municipal emergency alerts when severe weather is active

Verification Checklist

  1. Check the district home page and alert banner.
  2. Confirm transportation/bus service status.
  3. Check weather alerts in your exact location.
  4. Re-check close to the announced decision window.
Reminder: A prediction is not an announcement. When in doubt, rely on official district communications.
