    ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

    Is this an official closure announcement?

    No. This is an estimate built from forecast data. Official closure decisions come from your school board or transportation authority.

    What weather factors are included?

    Snow accumulation, snowfall rate, commute-hour timing, temperature, wind chill, wind speed, and low-visibility risk are all included in the scoring model.

    What locations are supported?

    You can enter Canadian postal codes and U.S. ZIP codes. District pages are available for selected regions listed in the schools directory.

    Where should I verify final status?

    Check official district channels, transportation websites, and emergency alerts. We maintain a source list at sources.html.
